About Our Community

More than a group. A family of learners.

Welcome to Science Buddies Family! 💙

A/L Science Buddies is a student learning community created for science stream students. Our aim is to provide a friendly environment where students can discuss Physics, Chemistry and Combined Mathematics doubts, share useful study materials and support one another throughout their educational journey.

Together, we learn. Together, we grow.
